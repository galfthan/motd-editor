Tip

Lights the area in the ink colour. Cells a line of another colour runs through are left as they are, so lines keep their colour.

Gives every cell the area reaches the paper colour. Lines keep their ink; in cells a line runs through, the paper shows on both sides of it.

Paper, then ink: no gaps along lines. With the paper at the terminal's colour it is the same as Ink.

The area spreads from the subpixel you click to the touching ones that look the same, and in an empty area also over the paper colour around drawn subpixels; other colours stop it. Characters (blocks, slants) count as ink: a fill started on ink recolours touching ones of its colour, and they stop a fill of an empty area.
